## Transit

Fairmont is on Airport T2. Metro Line 3 from the airport to BKC is about 5 minutes. The BKC station is 200 m–1.3 km from the Jio entrance. A car is 20–60 minutes. Grand Hyatt and Jio are different buildings.

- Fairmont → Jio, transit: https://www.google.com/maps/dir/?api=1&origin=Fairmont%20Mumbai&destination=Jio%20World%20Centre%20BKC&travelmode=transit
- Jio → Grand Hyatt: https://www.google.com/maps/dir/?api=1&origin=Jio%20World%20Centre%20BKC&destination=Grand%20Hyatt%20Mumbai%20BKC&travelmode=driving
- Fairmont → Nariman Point: https://www.google.com/maps/dir/?api=1&origin=Fairmont%20Mumbai&destination=Yashwantrao%20Chavan%20Centre%20Nariman%20Point&travelmode=driving
